# Forge · Play

### What you choose to make — SAFE-native craft under one bar.

> A forge is entered freely. Playground work graduates when it clears the bar: injected seams, own tests, own manifest, dependency-light core. The store keeps a ledger of stock; it does not keep two copies of the stock.

**Forge · Play** is the craft face of the fleet: incubating SAFE apps in a playground, promoting the ones that earn their own repo, and holding the bar every graduation must clear. Obligation lives elsewhere ([homestead-affairs](https://github.com/homestead-affairs)); here is workshop.

The live store and scaffold majors still live in the SAFE monorepo while promotion paths are wired. This org is the destination for graduated apps — one repo per standing product, not a shelf of duplicates.

---

## The two tiers

| Tier | What it means |
|------|----------------|
| **Playground** | Provisional, incubating, contested — a keeping record, not a second copy of the code |
| **Promoted** | Full SAFE app: own repo, injected storage, green tests, manifest, semantic-search seam |

Nestor and Jeles are the worked examples of promotion — lifted from inside a host into their own repos with injected storage and their own tests.

---

## The bar (short form)

A promoted app ships: **its own repository · injected seams (host imports it, never the reverse) · tests green · a manifest · a dependency-light core · MCP-shaped or library-clean · semantic search over its own injectable knowledge.**

See the store README in the SAFE monorepo for the full promotion checklist.
